Controls, checkpoints and game information

Arrow keys or WASD steer and drive; Space brakes, Escape pauses. Touch controls support holding steering and acceleration together. Click outside a form control before keyboard driving. Losing focus or hiding the tab pauses the game.

Personal bests and checkpoints are local device data. A checkpoint is compatible only with the same rules. No shared rankings, competitive verification, audio or multiplayer.
